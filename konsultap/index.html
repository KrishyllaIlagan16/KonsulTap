Privacy Notice

Draft for review. This text must be checked and approved by the hospital's Data Privacy Officer before real use.

What we collect

Your name, email, mobile number, the department, doctor, date and time you book, and the reason you give. Doctors record diagnoses and notes in your medical record during your visit.

Why we collect it

To book and confirm your consultation, manage the queue, email you your appointment details, and keep your medical record. Booking history without names is used to predict busy hours.

Who can see it

Administrators see bookings. A doctor sees the bookings assigned to them and patient records. Every access to patient records is logged. The public queue shows first names only.

Security

Passwords are stored as hashes, connections should use HTTPS, and staff access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission of serious breaches as the law requires.
